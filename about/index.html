About SpaceTerra

SpaceTerra is a star map, ISS tracker and live Earth app for Android, built and maintained by one developer.

Who builds SpaceTerra

I’m Radu Stavila, an Android developer. I design, build and support SpaceTerra myself, along with this website. I also make Xpiry, an expiry date tracker.

Where it started

SpaceTerra began in 2026 as a small, offline-first viewer for the astronomy picture of the day. It grew from there into the sky over your place, the missions exploring space and the events happening on Earth right now.

How SpaceTerra is made

  • Offline-first. What you have seen is kept on your phone, so the app opens fast and stays readable without a connection.
  • Your location stays local. There is no SpaceTerra account and no server holding your data. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what leaves your phone.
  • Built on open data. Pictures and data come from NASA, NOAA, USGS, ESA, JPL and other public sources, credited in the app. SpaceTerra is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
  • Free, with ads. Banner ads on some screens fund its development.
