Privacy Policy

SpaceTerra: Universe & Earth (“the app”) is made by Radu Stavila. This policy says what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. What stays on your device

  • Your place. The place you pick (or your device's location, if you allow it) is stored on your device and used there to work out the sky, passes, alerts and maps. It is never sent to us.
  • Your favourites, observing log and settings are stored on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may back them up to your Google account.
  • The camera (AR sky) is only shown on screen; nothing is recorded or saved.

2. What is sent to data providers

To show its content, the app downloads public data from providers such as NASA, NOAA, USGS, ESA/STScI, JPL, CelesTrak, Launch Library 2 and MET Norway. These requests carry your IP address, as any internet request does. For the stargazing forecast only, the app sends MET Norway your place rounded to about 1 km. No account, name or identifier is sent.

3. Usage statistics

With “Share usage statistics” on (Settings → Privacy, on by default), the app sends Google Analytics for Firebase anonymous counts of the screens and features you use. It never sends your location, place names, searches or anything you type. You can turn it off at any time.

4. Ads

From your third time opening the app, some screens show a banner ad. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may also show ads from Meta Audience Network through its auction. To show and measure ads, these networks may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with the ad.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first (Google's consent form) and loads no ads until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

How these companies use the data is described in their own policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
