1. The app

SpaceTerra shows the universe and Earth from public data: astronomy pictures, the sky over your place (sky map, AR view, sky events, satellite passes, comets, the Milky Way), launches, space weather and aurora, earthquakes, wildfires and other natural events, climate data, maps of Earth and the Moon, news and a daily quiz. It works offline from what it has already downloaded.

The app has no accounts: no registration, no username, no password. It is free to use and shows banner ads.

SpaceTerra is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or any other data provider. Their names appear only to credit where the data comes from.

2. Information, not a safety service

Everything in the app is for interest and education. It comes from third-party sources and the app's own calculations, may be late, incomplete or wrong, and may be shown from an older copy while you are offline.

  • Do not use the app to make safety decisions. Earthquake, wildfire and natural-event data, earthquake alerts, space weather and aurora alerts are not warnings. Follow your local authorities and official warning services.
  • Launch times, satellite passes, sky events, cloud forecasts and reminders are estimates. Notifications depend on your device's settings and Android may delay or drop them.
  • Never look at the Sun with your eyes, binoculars, a telescope or a camera without proper solar filters.
  • Use the AR sky view and maps only where it is safe: not while driving, cycling or walking in traffic. When observing, respect private property, local laws and your own safety outdoors at night.
