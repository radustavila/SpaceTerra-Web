Release notes

What changed in each version of SpaceTerra on Google Play. The newest version is at the top.

2026

Version 1.2.0 Latest November 5, 2026

  • Five new widgets: Moon, sky events, earthquakes, stargazing and the daily quiz
  • Every widget gets the new glass look
  • Long-press the app icon to jump to the sky map, Earth 3D, the ISS or the quiz
  • Tablets: cards side by side and more room for lists
  • Fixes and small improvements

Version 1.1.0 November 4, 2026

  • Live 3D globes of Earth and the Moon, with close-ups and today’s events
  • Wildfires around the world, for the past week or any day since 2021
  • Earthquakes over 30 days, coloured by your own rules, and searchable by place
  • Thousands more cities to pick your place from
  • Sky map: set the time, the place and what it shows in one sheet
  • A new Home with quick actions, a new search with voice, and a short welcome tour
  • Smoother maps, fixes and small improvements

Version 1.0.0 October 29, 2026

  • First release of SpaceTerra on Google Play
