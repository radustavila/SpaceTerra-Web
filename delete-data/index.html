Delete your data

SpaceTerra has no accounts and no server that holds your data. Your place, favourites, observing log and settings live on your phone, and the developer has no access to them.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

On your phone

Open Android Settings → Apps → SpaceTerra → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Either removes your place, favourites, observing log, settings and everything the app has cached.

Your Android backup

If Android backup is on, Android may have backed up your favourites and settings to your own Google account. You manage and delete that backup from your Google account, not from SpaceTerra.

Usage statistics

The app sends anonymous counts of the screens and features you use, never your location, place names, searches or anything you type. To stop it, turn off Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics in the app.

Ads

Ad networks may collect your device’s advertising ID. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, change your choice in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices in the app. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
